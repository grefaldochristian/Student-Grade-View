Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about using the grade portal.

How do I register?

Select Student, choose Sign Up, and enter your full name (first and last name), student ID (numbers only, like 2024-6348), program, year level and password.

How can I view my grades?

Log in with your registered student ID and password, then open View Grades. You can search, filter by semester or school year, print or export your grades.

How is the final grade computed?

The registrar encodes Prelim (30%), Midterm (30%) and Final exam (40%) scores. The portal computes the final percentage and converts it to the 1.00 - 5.00 scale automatically. Your GWA is the average of all grades weighted by subject units.

What does a passing grade mean?

Grades from 1.00 to 3.00 are passing. Grades above 3.00 are failing. Confirm the official grading policy with your registrar.

What is Dean's List standing?

In this portal, a weighted average of 1.75 or better with no grade lower than 2.50 shows as Dean's List.

Can I change my grades as a student?

No. Grade encoding is available only to registrar accounts. Students can update their own e-mail, mobile number and password from My Profile.

How is my account protected?

Passwords are stored as salted hashes, repeated wrong logins temporarily lock the form, and you are signed out after 15 minutes of inactivity. This is still a browser-only prototype.

Is this portal secure for official grades?

No. A front-end-only demonstration cannot securely protect academic records. Do not store confidential or official records in this prototype.

ABOUT THE PROJECT

About Veritas College of Irosin

Student Grade Viewing & Management Portal

Project Overview

The Student Grade Viewing & Management Portal is a demonstration web application designed to organize student information and academic grades through a simple interface.

Features

  • Student information management (add, update, delete).
  • Grade encoding with automatic final grade and GWA computation.
  • Searchable, filterable and sortable grade records.
  • Printable and exportable reports.
  • Secure-style login with lockout, hashed passwords and idle sign-out.
  • Responsive desktop and mobile layouts.

Technologies Used

HTML5, CSS3, Vanilla JavaScript, localStorage, and sessionStorage.

Data and Security Notice

This is a client-side educational prototype, not a production student information system. Data stored in browser storage is not protected like server-side records.
